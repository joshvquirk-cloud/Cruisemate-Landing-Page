Messaging Framework

Brand Voice

Tone

Direct, practical, confident. Speak to outcomes, not features.

Positioning

We solve "distribution failure, not information failure." Road changes are public. Getting them to you is the problem.

Emotional Anchors (Rev 2+)

• Relief: "You know before it affects you"

• Empowerment: "Stay ahead of disruption"

• Risk: Show what happens when you DON'T know (missed clients, delays, detours)

Key Phrases (Avoid)

• "Real-time" (we scan weekly, not live)

• "Never miss" (aspirational, not our guarantee)

• "Smart AI" (jargon; focus on outcomes)

Key Phrases (Use)

• "Know before..."

• "One email a week"

• "Permanent changes only"

• "Your location, your radius"

Revision 2 — PLANNED

Two variants:

V2.1 — Business (Refined)

  • Emotional messaging: "What happens when you don't know?"
  • Risk scenarios (missed clients, route delays, business disruption)
  • Relief copy ("Know before it hits you")
  • Cleaner pill grid layout
  • Bot status health badges (showing current council coverage)

V2.2 — Consumer (New)

  • Target: Moms, tradies, daily commuters, gig workers
  • Emotional hook: "Plan your day without surprises"
  • Use cases: "Missing school pickups," "Client appointments disrupted," "Finding alternate routes"
  • Simpler form (just email + suburb, no business info)
  • Map-based visualization of affected areas
  • SMS notification option (not just email)
