Overview

This changelog tracks all versions of the CruiseMate landing page, including design changes, feature updates, bug fixes, and known issues. Revisions are numbered sequentially. Two variants exist: Business (Revision 1) and Consumer (planned Revision 2.2).

Style Guide: All design decisions are locked in CruiseMate-Brand-Style-Guide.html. Reference before making visual changes.

Revision 1
Live 27 May 2026

Variant: Business

Primary landing page for businesses, trades, and logistics professionals.

What's Included

  • Dark green hero section with "KNOW BEFORE THE ROAD CHANGES" headline
  • Animated SVG: flowing cars and roads (4-directional intersection)
  • White signup card with 6-field form (business name, contact name, email, suburb, industry, alert radius)
  • 4-step "How It Works" cards
  • 12 notice type pills (lane closures, one-way streets, etc.)
  • Stats bar: 16 councils, 1 email/week, 100% free
  • Scroll reveal animations on sections
  • Formspree integration (form ID placeholder)
  • Mobile responsive (tested 768px breakpoint)
  • Safari compatibility (-webkit-backdrop-filter added)

Files

  • nsw-road-notices-landing.html — single-file, deploy-ready
  • CruiseMate-Brand-Style-Guide.html — design system reference

Revision 2.2
Planned TBD

Variant: Consumer (New)

Dedicated landing page for individuals, commuters, moms, gig workers, tradies (self-employed).

Key Differences from Rev 1

  • Emotional hook: "Plan your day without surprises"
  • Use cases: missing school pickups, client appointment delays, finding alternate routes
  • Simplified form: email + suburb only (no business info)
  • Map visualization showing affected areas in your radius
  • SMS notification option (in addition to email)
  • Warmer, less corporate tone in copy
  • Real-world scenarios instead of business talk

Design Changes

  • Hero may use different color (warmer green or secondary accent)
  • Icons for use cases (car, clock, family, briefcase)
  • Testimonials from consumers (commuters, parents, self-employed)
  • Notification preference toggle (email/SMS/both)
Target Audience

Moms, daily commuters, gig workers, self-employed tradies, delivery drivers

Unique Value

Personal route planning, not business operations
